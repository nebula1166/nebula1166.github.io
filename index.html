01 / ABOUT ME

Measuring deformation,
enabling intelligent touch.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Beihang University advised by Prof. Qinghua Wang. My work bridges experimental mechanics, optical full-field measurement and robot perception. I develop measurement methods for materials under complex conditions and explore visuotactile sensing for dexterous manipulation.

FINGERTIP VISUOTACTILE DEMO

Real-time fingertip tactile perception

Self-developed real-time fingertip visuotactile sensing based on a curved, monocular grayscale camera. The system captures contact-induced deformation for tactile perception and interaction.

02 / RESEARCH INTERESTS

Three connected research threads.

From material deformation to contact-rich robotic interaction.

01

Optical full-field measurement

Sampling moiré, grid methods and phase analysis for displacement and strain reconstruction.

02

Extreme-environment mechanics

High-temperature gratings and in-situ experiments for superalloy damage and crack evolution.

03

Visuotactile intelligence

Vision-based tactile sensing, contact-force reconstruction and tactile-visual policy learning.
